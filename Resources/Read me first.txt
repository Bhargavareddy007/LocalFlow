LocalFlow — voice typing that stays on your Mac
================================================

Needs: a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer), macOS 14 or newer, internet once (for a 669 MB speech model).

INSTALL
1. Drag LocalFlow.app into your Applications folder and double-click it.
2. A setup window appears. It downloads the speech model (one time) and asks for two permissions:
   - Microphone: click Allow.
   - Accessibility: click "Open System Settings", turn on LocalFlow in the list, come back.
3. When the window says "All set", hold the fn key, talk, and let go. The text appears where your cursor is.

TIPS
- Double-tap fn for hands-free mode (keeps listening); tap once to finish.
- Say "new line" or "new paragraph" to insert breaks.
- The waveform icon in the menu bar has all settings, your recent dictations, and a personal dictionary for names it gets wrong.
- Optional: "Enable polish wording" in the menu downloads a small local language model (1.1 GB) that fixes
  grammar and removes stumbles before typing. Still fully offline. Untick it any time to get your exact words.
- English and Greek are detected automatically. For Telugu or Hindi, switch to the Whisper engine in the menu.
- Your recent dictations are kept in the menu so you can copy them again; "Clear history" in that submenu
  deletes them. The app's log never contains what you said unless you turn that on for troubleshooting.
- Nothing you say ever leaves the Mac. Turn Wi-Fi off and it keeps working.
