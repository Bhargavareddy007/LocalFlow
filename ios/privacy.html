Privacy Policy

Local Flow for iPhone · Last updated 16 November 2026

Local Flow is a dictation app and keyboard for iPhone. It runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Local Flow has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting of its own and no servers. We do not receive, store or process any of your data.

Audio and text

  • Audio from the microphone is transcribed by a speech model running on your iPhone (NVIDIA Parakeet via whisper.cpp). Audio never leaves the phone and is never written to disk.
  • The text you dictate is kept only in the app's memory and is forgotten two minutes after you insert, copy or share it. The text reaches the Local Flow keyboard through a private container shared only between the app and its keyboard (an App Group), and is removed as soon as the keyboard has inserted it, or after two minutes at the latest.
  • When you choose a writing style other than "As spoken", the final text is rewritten by Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence). This also happens on your iPhone.
  • When you choose "Greek → English" or "English → Greek", the final text is translated by translation models built into the app (OPUS-MT), running on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere for this either.
  • While "Listening for the keyboard" is on, the app keeps the microphone open in the background so the keyboard can start a dictation without switching apps, and iOS shows the orange microphone indicator. Until you tap the keyboard's mic key, the audio is discarded as it arrives and nothing is kept or transcribed. You can turn this off, or have it switch off automatically after a period without dictation.
  • Words you add to the personal dictionary are stored on your iPhone only.

Network use

From version 1.2: none. Local Flow never connects to the internet. The speech and translation models are part of the app you download from the App Store, and neither the app nor its keyboard contains any networking code. It works the same in airplane mode.

Version 1.0 made one network request: a one-time download of the speech model file (about 670 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), pinned to an exact file version and verified against a checksum. Like any download, it showed your IP address to Hugging Face and contained no other information about you. Updating to 1.2 deletes that downloaded copy.

Full Access

iOS asks you to allow "Full Access" for the Local Flow keyboard. Local Flow needs it only so the keyboard can read the text the app hands back through the shared container. The keyboard does not use the network, does not collect what you type and does not store anything.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record your dictation on the device.
  • Background audio: to keep the microphone available for the keyboard, as described above.

Children

Local Flow does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is rated 4+.
